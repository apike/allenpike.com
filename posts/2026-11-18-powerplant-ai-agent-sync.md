Powerplant, our tool for improving AI dev workflows using coding agents' session traces, [is now open without an invite](https://usepowerplant.com/blog/powerplant-is-now-open/):

> Today we’re excited to announce that Powerplant is now available to all, and is free for teams under 25 seats.

Modern dev teams need to be able to leverage their coding agent sessions. These traces help make PRs more reviewable, improve [in-repo skills and instructions](https://allenpike.com/2026/in-repo-agent-instructions/), and can even explain why you're spending a face-melting amount of money on Claude Enterprise. But developers shouldn't need to push [every agent session irrevocably to git](https://entire.io) to get this benefit.

If that sounds appealing to you, [try Powerplant](https://usepowerplant.com/). Let me know how it could be better for your team, and we'll donate $50 to Rewiring America or Let's Encrypt. ⚡️
